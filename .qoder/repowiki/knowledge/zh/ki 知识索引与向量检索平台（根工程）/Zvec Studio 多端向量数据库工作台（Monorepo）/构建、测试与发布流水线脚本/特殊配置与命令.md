`python scripts/build_sidecar.py [--triple <rustc-triple>] [--skip-pyinstaller]` 需先 `make install.packaging` 且环境中有 `rustc`；`bash scripts/test_all.sh` 要求 `apps/backend/.venv` 已激活；`bash scripts/pre_release.sh` 和 `bash scripts/quality_report.sh` 会写结果到 `artifacts/` 目录；CI 中桌面构建前需安装 WebKit/GTK 系统依赖并 `cargo install tauri-driver`；Release 流水线在 macOS 上额外执行 `scripts/post-build-sign.sh` 对 .app 重签名。
